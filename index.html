Top Favorites (Books & Cinema)

A curated collection of inspirational books and films that continue to shape my perspective on technology, science, and creativity:

📚 Top 5 Favorite Books

  1. Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum (The Sealed Nectar) by Safiur Rahman Mubarakpuri – A definitive biographical study of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
  2. Bidayat al-Hidayah (The Beginning of Guidance) by Imam Al-Ghazali – A profound classical treatise on discipline, ethics, and spirituality.
  3. How to Prove It: A Structured Approach by Daniel J. Velleman – Essential foundation for transition mathematics and rigorous logic.
  4. Crime and Punishment by Fyodor Dostoevsky – A monumental psychological exploration of morality, guilt, and redemption.
  5. Can't Hurt Me: Master Your Mind and Defy the Odds by David Goggins – A powerful memoir on mental resilience, grit, and self-mastery.

🎬 Top 5 Favorite Movies

  • Interstellar (2014) – Directed by Christopher Nolan (A grand sci-fi odyssey through astrophysics, spacetime, and human love).
  • Se7en (1995) – Directed by David Fincher (A masterclass in tension, atmospheric neo-noir, and psychological investigative drama).
  • Fight Club (1999) – Directed by David Fincher (A subversive cult classic dissecting modern alienation and identity).
  • Dead Poets Society (1989) – Directed by Peter Weir (An unforgettable tribute to nonconformity, poetry, and carpe diem).
  • The Sixth Sense (1999) – Directed by M. Night Shyamalan (A legendary psychological thriller known for its narrative depth and iconic climax).
